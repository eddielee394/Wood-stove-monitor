# v0.10.0 planning: performance improvements (internal RAM)

Status: **planning**, nothing implemented yet. Written 2026-10-18 after the
v0.9.0 Home Assistant work.

## Why

Internal RAM is what Wi-Fi, TLS and DMA must use, and it is the tight
resource. In v0.9.0 we ran out of it: rebooting with the MQTT broker up ran
the boot-time weather fetch over HTTPS and the MQTT connect plus discovery
together. Internal RAM ran out and Wi-Fi died for good while the screen and
alarms ran on. v0.9.0 works around it: the two take turns, MQTT waits for
40 KB free, and the board restarts if RAM stays under 8 KB for 60 s. But
headroom is still thin:

- ~56 KB internal free in normal running.
- ~13 KB free at the low point, during the weather TLS fetch at boot.
- 2 MB PSRAM (slower, external) is almost unused.

## What is *not* the problem

**Fonts and icons use no RAM.** All five Plus Jakarta Sans fonts and every
icon total 67.5 KB, and all of it is in flash (`.flash.rodata`, addresses
`0x3C…`). Same for the web page (13.6 KB). `nm` labels them `d`, but
`objdump -t` shows they live in flash. Flash use is 1.4 MB of a 6.5 MB app
slot, so it isn't tight.

## Where internal RAM goes

| User | Internal RAM | Source |
|---|---|---|
| LVGL draw buffers, 2 × 480 px × 40 lines × 2 bytes | ~77 KB | Measured: `src/ui/ui.cpp`, `heap_caps_malloc(..., MALLOC_CAP_DMA \| MALLOC_CAP_INTERNAL)` |
| Wi-Fi driver and lwIP | ~50–60 KB | Typical for ESP32; precompiled, leave alone |
| Weather HTTPS (mbedTLS) | ~40 KB, only during a fetch | The 13 KB dip; precompiled, leave alone |
| Task stacks: weather 10 KB, net 6 KB, mqtt 6 KB, sensors 4 KB, alarms 4 KB, loop 8 KB, plus system tasks | ~45 KB | From `xTaskCreatePinnedToCore` calls; probably oversized |
| Static `.data` + `.bss` (CA cert, MQTT publish buffer, library tables) | ~55 KB | Measured from the ELF |
| LVGL widgets (labels, rows, tabs; small mallocs stay internal) | Unknown, likely 20–40 KB | Needs measuring |

## Plan, biggest gain first

### 1. Shrink the LVGL draw buffers: 38–58 KB back
- Options: two 20-line buffers (saves ~38 KB), or one 20-line buffer (saves
  ~58 KB, no flush/render overlap).
- Cost: slower full-screen redraws (screen changes, settings tabs). The
  dashboard updates a few small areas once a second, so it shouldn't notice.
- Test both on the device. Eddie judges screen switching and touch response
  by eye.
- Alternative to try: buffers in PSRAM. Check whether LovyanGFX's i80 bus
  can DMA from PSRAM on the S3 without a big slowdown.

### 2. Memory diagnostics in Home Assistant (do first, for before/after numbers)
- New diagnostic sensors:
  - free internal RAM;
  - lowest free since boot (`heap_caps_get_minimum_free_size`);
  - largest free block (`heap_caps_get_largest_free_block`), which predicts
    TLS failures better than the total, since TLS needs contiguous blocks;
  - free PSRAM.
- Also in `/api/status`.
- Add them to the History → Monitor health card in `docs/ha-dashboard.yaml`.
- Watch them across a real burn before and after the changes.

### 3. Right-size task stacks: ~10–15 KB back
- Log `uxTaskGetStackHighWaterMark` per task (console command and/or a
  one-off diagnostic). Exercise every path first: weather fetch, MQTT
  discovery, OTA, the Wi-Fi scan on the setup screen.
- Trim each stack to its peak plus a safety margin (~1–1.5 KB). Never cut
  the sensor or alarm task close; they are the safety path.

### 4. Move non-critical buffers to PSRAM: a few KB, less fragmentation
- ArduinoJson documents (MQTT state and discovery, weather parse): give
  them a PSRAM allocator.
- PubSubClient's 2 KB buffer and the `publishJson` static buffer.
- The `/api/status` JSON `String`.
- They are small but churned constantly, which fragments internal RAM over
  time and shrinks the largest free block.

### Not planned
- Rebuilding the framework to shrink Wi-Fi or mbedTLS buffers (would mean
  leaving the precompiled Arduino core). Revisit only if 1–4 aren't enough.

## Done when
- Lowest free internal RAM since boot stays above ~40 KB through a full
  burn, including weather fetches, MQTT reconnects and an OTA.
- A restart with the broker up connects without hitting the turn-taking
  limits.
- Screen switching still feels fine to Eddie.
